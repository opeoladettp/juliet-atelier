CULTURAL STORYTELLER

Bridging Heritage and Global Couture

Founded and led by Ocheibi Onyanta Juliet, Onyeodime Atelier operates at the unique intersection of indigenous craftsmanship and global high fashion.

With a strong foundation in Business Administration from the Federal Polytechnic Bauchi, Juliet combines sharp design leadership with strategic business acumen. She oversees the end-to-end production lifecycle—from concept development and sustainable textile sourcing to runway presentations and client styling.

"We build scalable fashion systems that preserve the soul of African weaving and dyeing while presenting it to the international stage."
— Ocheibi Onyanta Juliet, Creative Director

SOCIAL IMPACT

Empowering Local Artisanship

We believe luxury should empower. Onyeodime Atelier is built on a structured production model that provides employment to over 10 native artisans, weavers, and tailors within the regional fashion value chain.

By creating stable economic opportunities and showcasing their handiwork at showcases like Africa Fashion Week London and Lagos Fashion Week, we promote cultural sustainability and support traditional craftsmanship.
